EXAM SKILLS

Essay practice

Plan a balanced argument before writing. Use precise evidence and evaluate the significance of each point.

PRACTICE QUESTION

Evaluate the view that Parliament is effective at scrutinising the government.

Planning framework

  1. Judgement: define what “effective” means and give your overall line.
  2. Argument for: explain a scrutiny mechanism and use a specific example.
  3. Argument against: explain a limitation, such as government control of the Commons timetable or party discipline.
  4. Weigh it up: compare how far each argument affects government accountability.
  5. Conclusion: reach a clear, qualified judgement that answers the wording.
